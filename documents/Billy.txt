Billy is a Hero in Deadlock . A white hot ball of rage, furious at the very notion of... something. Billy is a chaotic front-liner who never backs down from a challenge. With Rising Ram he gets up close and personal, before slamming down with Bashdown to create shockwaves through his enemies. Running isn't even an option when Billy can use his ultimate Chain Gang to grapple enemies and pull them back in. A quick swig from his bottle gets him Blasted, and his enemies totally wrecked.
From looting to public vandalism, Billy makes sure anyone who can be bothered to listen gets to hear "the truth". Don't ask him how he got the goat head.
Billy was released on August 20, 2025 as the second hero of the Six New Heroes update (first winner of hero vote).

## SECTION: Innate
Billy has a passive that alters his light melee attacks . He can use light melees faster than other heroes (shorter recovery time), gets slowed less when using light melees, and his reload is not interrupted by light melees. This allows Billy to hit the Sinner's Sacrifice jackpot faster than other heroes if he uses only light melee.
Additionally, he can restore ammo when hitting melees once he unlocks his Blasted ability, as detailed below.

## SECTION: Weapon
Due to Billy's Blasted passive, hitting a melee attack reloads his ammo by either 100% (heavy melee) or 35% (light melee).

## SECTION: Bashdown
BASHDOWN 0.3 s 4 m 1 8 s 35 s
Slam your bat into the ground, pulling enemies down and dealing melee damage . The slam creates a shockwave that deals spirit damage and applies knockup .
Upgrades: 1 -10s Cooldown ; 2 +1 Charge and +2m Radius ; 5 -3s Charge Time Now deals 60% Heavy Melee Damage

## SECTION: Rising Ram
RISING RAM 0.3 s 32 s
Charge head-first into an enemy and send them into the air along with Billy. Cooldown reduced by 50% on impact.
Upgrades: 1 On Impact: +25% weapon damage for 5s ; 2 0.4s Charge Duration ; 5 10 % Max Health Damage -13 s Cooldown

## SECTION: Blasted
BLASTED 8 s 27 s
Passive: Melee hits restore ammo and inflict wrecked on the victim for 7s.
Active: Bullets are amplified against wrecked enemies. Using melee boosts max health and extends duration.
Upgrades: 1 While Blasted: +2.25m/s bonus move speed ; 2 +1 Bashdown Charge on use and +7% Wrecked Bullet Amp ; 5 +50 Melee Bonus Health and increases spirit scaling

## SECTION: Chain Gang
CHAIN GANG 12 m 2.8 s 150 s
Chain nearby enemies to you. Chained enemies cannot use movement abilities and receive a heavy slow when they pull on the chain. After a delay, yank everyone towards Billy and deal spirit damage . The chain breaks if you lose line of sight for a short while.
Upgrades: 1 +40% spirit resist & bullet resist ; 2 -50 s Cooldown ; 5 +5m Range +1.3s of Unstoppable Per Hero pulled in

## SECTION: Trivia
- " Billy goat " is a term used for a male goat.
- Billy's weapon name, Biquette , means "young female goat" in French. It is a reference to " Biquette the punk rock goat ", who went viral on the internet for hanging around punk rock and metal concerts.
- Billy has unused "bespoke" voicelines that make meta references to the game, such as the popular melee Abrams build, calling McGinnis ' turrets "aimbots", and calling out Haze for "pressing 4". Billy makes a reference to the Rejuvenator rework, saying that "it used to go out to everybody until the Man decided it was too good".
- Billy was one of the heroes in a private build who was accidentally leaked on stream in May 2025. Being early in development, he was using Shiv 's model as a placeholder. Billy's work-in-progress portrait was later leaked by an anonymous Discord user in July 2025.
- "Ranting in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Billy in the Hideout .
- One of Billy's in-game hero select screen labels is G.O.A.T., a common acronym standing for "greatest of all time".
- Wrecked was originally called 'Pissed', remnants of this can still be seen while hovering over the icon next to the healthbar while Billy is Blasted.
- Billy had the largest lead in the Six New Heroes contest, at 30%.
- Billy possesses a full set of teeth at the front of his mouth. This is unlike real goats, who lack an upper set and instead have a hard dental pad.
- Billy eats his gun magazine in his reload animation. This is a reference to the popular myth that goats can consume and digest any material, including inedible ones.
- Billy does a sign of the horns when riding the zipline. This can be a reference to both his actual goat horns and his love of punk music, as well as a common salute in Satanism and occult practices.
