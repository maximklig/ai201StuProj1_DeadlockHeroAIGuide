Paradox is a Hero in Deadlock . She wields time manipulation with potent disruption and slowing abilities. With a charged Kinetic Carbine, she can threaten her enemies at any range while damaging and slowing them with thrown Pulse Grenades and well-placed Time Walls. At just the right moment, Paradox can activate her Paradoxical Swap to change places with her target, dropping them into a dangerous situation amongst her team.
Paradox is both the name of this Hero and the name of their Thieves' Guild, specializing in stealing the untouchable and breaking into ultra-secure facilities. Their motives are unclear, except for a certain level of delight that its members seem to take in snubbing the world's biggest corporations and governments.

## SECTION: Pulse Grenade
PULSE GRENADE 5.5 m 3.2 s 32 s
Throw a grenade that begins pulsing when it lands. Each pulse expands the radius and applies spirit damage , time slow, and stacking increased damage for Paradox against the victim.
Upgrades: 1 -12 s Cooldown ; 2 +20 Pulse Damage and increased Spirit scaling ; 5 +4% Damage Amp and +1.6s Duration

## SECTION: Time Wall
TIME WALL 5.08 m 5.5 s 25 s
Create a time warping wall that stops time for all enemy projectiles and bullets that touch it and increases the speed and damage of friendly bullets. Enemies that touch the wall will be briefly slowed and silenced .
Upgrades: 1 +3.5s Duration +3m Width +1m Height ; 2 2.3s Silence Duration +35% Weapon Damage ; 5 +2 Ability Charges

## SECTION: Kinetic Carbine
KINETIC CARBINE 28 s
Charge up a powerful shot of time energy, dealing spirit damage and applying a Time-Stop to enemies hit. The damage dealt increases with weapon damage . Move speed is increased while charging. While in the air and charging, Zoom / alt-fire to timeslow your movement.
Upgrades: 1 0.4 s Max Time-Stop ; 2 -12 s Cooldown ; 5 +2s Charge Hold Duration and +55% Max Damage Scaling

## SECTION: Paradoxical Swap
PARADOXICAL SWAP 25 m 95 s
Fire a projectile that swaps your position with the target enemy hero.
Upgrades: 1 200 Barrier for 8s. Barrier scales with Spirit ; 2 -35s Cooldown and +13m Cast Range ; 5 Swaps all enemies within 7m of hit target and +10% Max Health Damage

## SECTION: Trivia
- Paradox was named Chrono in Neon Prime .
- Paradox is voiced by Elle Newlands, who also voiced Horizon in Apex Legends . Both characters are Scottish and use technology of spacetime manipulation.
- Paradox wears a blue rose in her lapel, which do not exist in nature, and are only possible through genetic modification. In art, blue roses are a symbol for mystery and the unattainable.
- "Scheming in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Paradox in the Hideout .
