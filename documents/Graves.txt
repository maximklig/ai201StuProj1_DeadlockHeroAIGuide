Graves is a Hero in Deadlock . The dead have been making a whole lot of demands lately, and only Graves has the courage to oblige. That severed lich hand she found in the cemetery has proven itself a valuable ally, allowing her to latch onto her enemies and steal their power for herself. Her curiosities about the passage of life are further put to use with Jar of Dead, collecting the remains of the fallen, shaking them up, and throwing them back at her enemies to grant those remorseful spirits a final shot at revenge. With Grasping Hands she strikes a deep crack in the earth, allowing those down below to grab onto enemies' ankles and stop them from escaping her reach. And when all else fails, a Borrowed Decree will summon the dead to fight alongside her.
For Darcy Graves, the cemetery was a welcomed respite from her home life. So when she unearthed a lich hand buried in Van Courtland Park, Graves found the best necromancy teacher that she could ask for.
Graves was released on January 29, 2026 as the second winner of the Old Gods, New Blood update contest.

The Teacher attaches to the target closest to the reticle. It cannot deal headshots and Graves cannot buy headshot items.

## SECTION: Jar of Dead
JAR OF DEAD 4 13 s
Passive: Collect death when anything dies nearby and store it in your Jar of Dead. Active: Throw a jar to summon Deadheads that repeatedly deal spirit damage to enemies. Deadheads follow you instead if there's no nearby enemies, and prioritize the target of your weapon.
Upgrades: 1 Collecting death heals 5 health per pickup ; 2 On Hit: Apply 30% Slow for 1s ; 5 +2 Deadheads +4s Deadhead Lifetime

## SECTION: Grasping Hands
GRASPING HANDS 24 m 5 s 34 s
Unearth a line of grasping hands, summoning a Ghoul and leaving behind a rift. Enemies who pass through take spirit damage and receive immobilize . Alt-Cast Alternate cast rotates the orientation of the wall.
Upgrades: 1 2 s Duration ; 2 +90 Damage and +10m Wall Length ; 5 +1 Ghouls Raised -10s Cooldown +1s Immobilize Duration

## SECTION: Essence Theft
ESSENCE THEFT 4 s
Your weapon steals weapon damage and spirit resist over time, up to a maximum amount per target.
Upgrades: 1 5 % Max Spirit Resist Stolen ; 2 20 % Max Weapon Damage Stolen ; 5 All Summons now apply Essence Theft

## SECTION: Borrowed Decree
