Infernus is a Hero in Deadlock . He is an Ixian with a powerful control of flame that he uses to enhance his speed and ignite his enemies. Combining his Flame Dash with the ability to spew a Napalm catalyst, Infernus can chase down and ignite his foes with a deadly Afterburn. Any targets left standing will be subject to his explosive Concussive Combustion, knocking them out and leaving them vulnerable to further immolation.
As a creature from Ixia, Infernus had a troubled and intense youth while he was raised in the United States. Despite a rash of deadly crime as a teenager, he found himself tending a bar in his adulthood and learning to calm his intense emotions. With quality music and interesting people to talk to, Infernus rarely has a need these days to call up his dangerous supernatural abilities.

## SECTION: Flame Dash
FLAME DASH 3 s 38 s
Dash forward, gaining slow resistance while leaving a flaming trail that deals spirit damage over time . Hold Move forward while active to dash farther.
Upgrades: 1 -12 s Cooldown ; 2 +1s Trail Duration and +20 DPS ; 5 Enable 2 Ability Charges with 14s Recharge time

## SECTION: Afterburn
AFTERBURN
Weapon hits build up a burning effect, dealing spirit damage over time . Abilities refresh the base burn duration.
Upgrades: 1 16 Damage Per Second ; 2 Burn Effect: -35% Spirit Damage ; 5 +3s Max Burn Duration

## SECTION: Concussive Combustion
CONCUSSIVE COMBUSTION 12 m 165 s
Become a living bomb, dealing spirit damage and applying stun to all nearby enemies after a delay. Once cast, Concussive Combustion cannot be interrupted.
Upgrades: 1 100 Damage ; 2 -75s Cooldown and +100% Explosion Lifesteal ; 5 +0.9s Stun Duration and +10m Radius

- Infernus had a tail in The Binding of Oathkeeper artwork and in one of his old models.
