Wraith is a Hero in Deadlock . She is a fast-talking and fast-firing combatant with potent offensive abilities and mobility. She can activate her Full Auto to boost her own fire rate, perform Card Tricks by sending them at her targets and Project Mind to teleport either into or out of dangerous fights. Singling out a foe with her Telekinesis and throwing them into any direction, Wraith can decide who on the enemy team should die first.
Employing her charisma and strength to run a gambling network, Wraith is a widely-known feature of the criminal underworld of New York City.

## SECTION: Card Trick
CARD TRICK 500 m 4 m 2 0.6 s
Deal weapon damage to summon cards of a random suit. Each card can be thrown, dealing damage and applying a different effect depending of its suit. Cards thrown fly towards the enemy or point under your crosshair. Alt-cast to throw first card
Each suit provides a different bonus. Jokers counts as all suits
Upgrades: 1 2 Charges ; 2 +40 Damage and increased Spirit Scaling ; 5 All suits are improved and higher chance for Jokers

## SECTION: Trivia
- Wraith's hands are always inside her pockets, as her two magic arms perform actions for her. Wraith's current character model doesn't actually have hands. However, there is a work-in-progress Wraith model in the files that has fully modeled arms and hands, as well as new animations performed with both her real arms and magic arms.
- Wraith has had 4 iterations of different designs and voice actors since her creation. One of her earlier designs was a magician. That model was reused for the Metal Skin poster and for Sinclair 's character model.
- Wraith's weapon was originally called Houdini's Shackles .
- Wraith's weapon is based on the Thompson submachine gun (AKA "Tommy Gun"), notable for its usage by organized crime syndicates in the early 20th century.
- Wraith's ammo clip size is 52 as a reference to the number of cards in a poker deck . Her original clip size was 50. It was changed based on a player's suggestion in the official Deadlock Discord.
- Removed Patron voice lines indicate that Wraith's previous backstory involved a search for her father, who had suffered a mysterious fate.
- "Gambling in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Wraith in the Hideout .
- Wraith's title uses the Storica font.
