Kelvin is a Hero in Deadlock . He is a dedicated, undead scientist with a bevy of freezing abilities to slow his enemies and heal allies. He can conjure an Ice Path under his feet for faster movement for himself and his team, throwing Frost Grenades and firing his Arctic Beam at nearby enemies to set them up for his companions' attacks. Tactical usage of his Frozen Shelter ice dome can protect and heal allies while trapping hapless opponents.
As an adventuring scientist, Kelvin was particularly excited for rumors of an 8th Astral Gate opening in the Arctic, developing an expedition to hunt for it. However, something went wrong with the expedition, vanishing into the snow and leaving Kelvin's frozen, animate body to be discovered over a year later. With no memory of the expedition, Kelvin seeks the Patrons for answers.

## SECTION: Frost Grenade
FROST GRENADE 6.5 m 2 7 s 30 s
Throw a grenade that explodes in a cloud of freezing ice that heals allies and applies spirit damage and move speed reduction to enemies.
Upgrades: 1 +30 Damage and +30 Heal Amount ; 2 -10 Cooldown and debuff now freezes Stamina Regen ; 5 +2m Radius. Damage and Heal Spirit Scaling increased.

## SECTION: Ice Path
ICE PATH 8 s 50 s
Kelvin creates a floating trail of ice and snow that gives movement bonuses to him and his allies. Kelvin gains 60% slow resistance for the duration. Enemies can also walk on the floating trail. Press Dash / Crouch / slide to travel up or down while in Ice Path.
Upgrades: 1 +2m Move Speed and +35% Bullet Resist for Kelvin while on Ice Path ; 2 -25 s Cooldown ; 5 +35% Spirit and +20 Spirit for Kelvin while on Ice Path

## SECTION: Arctic Beam
ARCTIC BEAM 5 s 28 s
Shoot freezing cold energy out in front of you, damaging targets and progressively reducing their movement and fire rate the longer you sustain the beam on them. You have reduced move speed while using Arctic Beam. The beam may also claim Souls.
Upgrades: 1 -25% to Fire Rate and Move Speed ; 2 +20 DPS and increased Spirit Scaling ; 5 -13s Cooldown and Fires 2 additional Arctic Beams within 10m of the last target hit.

## SECTION: Frozen Shelter
FROZEN SHELTER 8 m 10 m 5 s 170 s
Target yourself or a Hero to freeze the air and create an impenetrable dome around them. While in the dome, allies gain rapid regeneration and enemies are slowed . Objectives becomes Invulnerable and Frozen under Frozen Shelter
Upgrades: 1 -25 s Cooldown ; 2 1.5 s Duration ; 5 Purges non-ult debuffs for you and your allies. +65 Health Regen that now scales with Spirit
