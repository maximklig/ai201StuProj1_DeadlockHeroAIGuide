Apollo is a Hero in Deadlock . With Flawless Advance the fencer begins his approach, timing his strikes perfectly to inflict more damage. Counter-attacks aren't a concern for Apollo, as a flourish of his blade deflects the damage and triggers a quick Riposte, and Disengaging Sigil allows him to jump out of the fray with ease. For his final blow, Apollo dons his fencer's mask and channels Ixia 's might with Itani Lo Sahn, leaving enemies defeated in his wake.
As the prince of Ixia , Apollo comes from a land of beauty, tradition and conflict. Political violence was common in Ixia, but when Apollo was sent to New York for his own safety, he knew the situation was serious. Now a highly skilled fencer at Blackmore Academy , Apollo knows that in due time, he will be called back to Ixia... and when he returns, he will show why he's worthy to wield his family's ancestral blade.
Apollo was released on February 12, 2026 as the last hero of the Old Gods, New Blood update.

## SECTION: Weapon
Pride of Ixia has no damage falloff and deals no damage beyond its max range.

## SECTION: Disengaging Sigil
DISENGAGING SIGIL 12 s
Draw a sigil sphere in front of you and then leap backwards as it explodes, damaging and slowing enemies caught in it.
Upgrades: 1 30 Damage ; 2 +30% Fire Rate and +50% Bullet Speed for 10s ; 5 On Player Hit: +2 stamina restored and resets Air Jump/Dash limit

## SECTION: Riposte
RIPOSTE 0.8 s 22 s
Prepare to deflect the next incoming attack. On a successful deflection, briefly become invulnerable and target an enemy hero to dash towards them, stunning them and reducing their Melee Resist . Press Ability 2 to select a target. Does not trigger against trooper or neutral damage.
Upgrades: 1 -7 s Cooldown ; 2 -30 % Melee Resist ; 5 1.6 s Stun Duration

## SECTION: Flawless Advance
FLAWLESS ADVANCE 8 s 26 s
Perform a series of lunges in any direction, delivering piercing stabs ahead of you. Hold your ability key to time your attacks, dealing more damage the longer it's held. Releasing your attack during the perfect window deals maximum damage . Press Ability 3 to re-cast.
Upgrades: 1 Perfect Hit on Hero: Heal 35 ; 2 -12s Cooldown. Gain +60% Bullet Resist during lunge ; 5 +65 Perfect Damage and increased spirit scaling. Increased lunge attacking dash speed & distance.

## SECTION: Itani Lo Sahn
ITANI LO SAHN 125 s
Charge up and perform a long range slash. Struck enemies cannot take action or heal and are stuck in slow motion. When this effect expires, they suffer devastating damage , dealing bonus damage against half-health enemies. While in slow motion, Apollo is invulnerable and enemies take reduced damage. Hold Ability 4 or Fire to delay the cast.
Upgrades: 1 8 m Dash Range ; 2 -40 s Cooldown ; 5 +40 % Bonus Damage

## SECTION: Trivia
- Apollo's ultimate "Itani Lo Sahn" is Ixian for "I have no equal" .
- Apollo was first teased in the Hideout with a rapier over the item shop.
- A possible inspiration for Apollo is the hero concept Fencer (Apollo's original codename) by player nathanieljbrill in the Deadlock forums. The Fencer concept used a sword as the primary weapon and had the abilities to slow enemies, deflect incoming attacks, and use a charged attack.
- "Striving Towards Perfection in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Apollo in the Hideout .
